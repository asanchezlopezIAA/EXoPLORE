## WASP-127 b equatorial jet

The resolved double-limb (morning/evening) signature illustrated in the Concepts
primer and in Tutorial 3 is of the kind detected in WASP-127 b.

> Nortmann, L., Lesjak, F., Yan, F., et al. (2025).
> *CRIRES+ transmission spectroscopy of WASP-127 b. Detection of the resolved
> signatures of a supersonic equatorial jet and cool poles in a hot planet.*
> A&A, 693, A213.
> [doi:10.1051/0004-6361/202450438](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202450438)
